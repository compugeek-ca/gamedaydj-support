Privacy Policy

Gameday DJ · Last updated November 19, 2026

← Gameday DJ Support

In short

  • We don't collect any personal data. Gameday DJ has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • Your music, playlists, recordings and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The internet is used only when you ask for it: to look up a song's details or cover, or to download additional announcer voices.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by the app store. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, and never have.

Who we are

Gameday DJ (the "app") is made by CompuGeek.ca ("we", "us") in Canada. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and this website. Questions about it go to info@compugeek.ca.

Information that stays on your device

To do its job, the app keeps the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

  • Your songs and playlists: the buttons, playlists, start and end times, horns, display names and settings you set up, and which songs you've played recently (so they aren't repeated too soon).
  • Music access: if you allow it, the app reads songs from your device's music library, and from folders you choose to link. It only reads them to play them.
  • Recordings: if you allow microphone access, voice-overs and announcements you record are saved in the app. The microphone is used only while you're recording.
  • Images: a team logo or song cover you choose from your photos or files.
  • Announcements and your roster: the players' names and numbers you enter or import, and the announcement audio generated from them. Voices are generated on your device.
  • Whether you've unlocked premium.

Backups are files you create yourself (Settings → Back up) and save where you choose, such as the Files app, a file manager or a cloud drive. Wherever you save one, that service's own privacy policy applies.

When the app uses the internet

Gameday DJ works without an internet connection. It connects only when you use one of these features, and sends only what's needed to answer:

When you…What's sentTo
Use Fix metadata or Search for a song's details or cover The song's title and artist (or words from its file name) MusicBrainz, the Cover Art Archive and Apple's iTunes Search
Type a link to a cover imageA request for that linkThe website you typed
Tap Download additional voicesNothing of yours; the voices are downloaded once Hugging Face

These requests use secure connections (HTTPS) and don't include any account, name, contact details or device identifier from the app. As with any website you visit, these services see your internet (IP) address when you connect; their own privacy policies, linked above, cover what they do with it. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Gameday DJ is free for up to 20 songs, and a one-time in-app purchase unlocks premium. The purchase, and restoring it, are handled entirely by the app store you downloaded Gameday DJ from, under that store's privacy policy: Apple's App Store, Google Play or the Amazon Appstore. We don't receive your name, email, account or payment details. The app only asks the store whether premium is unlocked, and remembers the answer on your device.

For purchases made through Apple, the App Store Terms of Use (EULA) apply.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to help you, and never share or sell it. Ask us any time to delete it.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking of our own. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

Gameday DJ is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We don't knowingly receive personal information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, including under Canada's PIPEDA and Alberta's PIPA, the EU and UK GDPR, and California's CCPA/CPRA, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Since the app sends us nothing, the only information we could hold is an email you've sent us. Write to info@compugeek.ca and we'll reply within 30 days.

We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising or profiling. You may also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or Alberta's Information and Privacy Commissioner.

Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode and encryption. The internet requests described above use secure connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change ever means the app starts collecting personal information, we'll say so in the app before it happens.
